Shukrawar Dandruff & Scalp Check Check Day

Raviwar Wash Day 2 (Full Clean) Wash Day

Shampoo + Conditioner lagao. Important: Towel se ragdo mat! Sirf halka thap-thapa kar sukhao ya normal hawa mein sukhne do.

1. Nahane se pehle Nariyal Tel kyun zaroori hai?

Jab baal geele hote hain toh paani sokh kar phool jaate hain aur kamzor ho jaate hain. Nariyal tel baalon ke andar ghus jaata hai aur baalon ko tootte se bachata hai.

2. Rosemary Oil kyun lagana hai?

Rosemary tel sir ki chamdi mein blood flow badhata hai. Isse baal tezi se aur ghane grow hote hain (bina kisi side-effect ke).

3. Shampoo roz kyun nahi lagana?

Roz shampoo lagane se sir ka natural oil nikal jaata hai, aur baal jhadu jaise sukhe aur frizzy ban jaate hain. Hafte mein sirf 2 baar shampoo kaafi hai.

4. Shampoo aur Conditioner ka sahi tareeqa:

Shampoo sirf sir ki chamdi (scalp) saaf karne ke liye hota hai. Conditioner sirf baalon ko soft rakhne ke liye hota hai (isliye conditioner ko chamdi par mat lagao, sirf baalon par lagao).

Month 1 se 3 (Start)
Baal badhna shuru honge

Baal kaano tak aane lagenge aur thode bikhre lagenge. Karna kya hai: Upar ke baal bilkul mat kato. Sirf gardan ke pichhe se safai karwao.

Month 4 se 7 (Awkward Stage)
Baal ajeeb lagenge (Sabse mushkil time)

Baal na chote lagenge na poore lambe, phoolne lagenge. Karna kya hai: Haar mat maanna! Cap, hairband use karo ya baal pichhe set karo. Barber ke paas jakar gusse mein chota mat karwana.

Month 8 se 12 (Photo 2 Look)
Photo 2 jaisa final look!

Baal hothon aur chin tak pahunch jayenge. Kisi achhe salon mein jao, photo 2 dikhao aur bolo: "Sirf layers daal kar flow set kar do, length kam mat karna."

Jab bhi salon/barber ke paas jao, bas yeh dialogue bolna taaki wo aapki 6 mahine ki mehnat na kaat de:

"Bhaiya, main baal lambe kar raha hoon (growing them out). Upar ki length ko haath bhi mat lagana. Sirf gardan (neckline) aur kaano ke paas se halka clean kar do aur kharab ends trim kar do."
