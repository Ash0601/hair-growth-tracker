1. Nahane se pehle Nariyal Tel kyun lagana hai?

Baal geele hone par kamzor ho jaate hain. Nariyal tel baalon ke andar ghus kar unhe tootte se bachata hai.

2. Rosemary Oil kyun?

Rosemary tel blood circulation badhata hai, jisse baal tezi se aur ghane grow hote hain.

3. Roz shampoo kyun nahi lagana?

Roz shampoo lagane se sir ka natural tel chala jaata hai, aur baal sukhe aur frizzy ho jaate hain.

4. Shampoo vs Conditioner ka sahi tareeqa:

Shampoo sirf sir ki chamdi (scalp) ke liye hai. Conditioner sirf baalon ko soft rakhne ke liye hai (isliye conditioner ko chamdi par mat lagao).
